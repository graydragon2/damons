Our Story

A friendly small-town place to eat

For over two decades, Damon's has been the spot Jesup locals count on for hand-breaded chicken fingers, saucy wings and burgers made to order. We're a family-run kitchen that believes good food and good people go together.

  • Wings & fingers in Mild, Wild, Inferno, Insane, BBQ, Teriyaki & Naked
  • Burgers, Fresh Mex, salads and sandwiches for the whole family
  • Locally owned and serving Jesup, GA since 1999
